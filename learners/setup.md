## Signing into GitLab

You will need to sign into [ORNL's GitLab](https://code.ornl.gov) to follow episodes 7 & 8 in this lesson.

1. Go to <https://code.ornl.gov> and follow the prompts to sign-in.

## Preparing Your Working Directory

We'll do our work in the `Desktop` folder so make sure you change your working directory to it with:

```bash
$ cd
$ cd Desktop
```
